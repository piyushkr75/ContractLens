# ContractLens — Demo Test Questions

## 1. Contract Q&A
1. Can either party terminate the agreement without cause?
2. What are the payment terms and late-payment interest?
3. Does the contract automatically renew?
4. What is the initial contract duration?

## 2. Risk Analysis
5. Does the contract specify a maximum liability amount?
6. What data breach notification deadline is specified?
7. Does the agreement clearly define ownership of custom-developed work?
8. Are the indemnification and dispute resolution provisions sufficiently detailed for review?

## 3. Missing Information
9. What governing law applies?
10. Does the agreement specify a guaranteed uptime percentage?
11. What is the data retention period after termination?

## 4. Calculations
12. What is the total recurring service fee for 12 months, excluding the implementation fee?
13. What is the total fee for the initial 12 months, including the one-time implementation fee?

## 5. Evidence and Hallucination Testing
14. Answer the questions using contract evidence and provide page references.
15. If the contract does not contain an answer, explicitly state that the information is not specified.
16. Identify conflicting clauses, if any, and cite the supporting text.
